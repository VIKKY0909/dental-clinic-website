Free Airport Transfer

With more than 24 years in the field, we at Kadali Dental are aware of the needs and wants of international patients.

For comfort and convenience of our patients Kadali Dental provides 24x7 complimentary airports pick up and drop facility. At Kadali's, We understand this apprehension that traveling to a new country and commuting is the biggest hurdle. Making your visit hassle free once you have your travel dates and flight time confirmed with the clinic our dedicated driver will be there at the airport arrival to welcome you holding placard of your name. Upon arrival, the guests would be dropped straight to Kadali Dental /Hotel as per the time of the arrival. At Kadali Dental we give free transport from the Hotels mentioned below to our guests during treatment irrespective of the number of appointments.
